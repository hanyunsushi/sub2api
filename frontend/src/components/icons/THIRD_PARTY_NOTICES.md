## Apple SF Symbols

All original SF Symbols artwork remains subject to Apple's applicable license. This application integration does not grant a new license to Apple artwork and does not imply Apple endorsement. These glyphs are not used as logos or App icons.

Regular glyph contours are taken from the data-only exports of `bradleyhodges/sfsymbols`, pinned to `2a0100e93e11a22ea0ff5e0530efd10e2cf0d243`. Source URLs and SHA-256 of each original export are recorded in `sf-symbols.json`. The export metadata describes the upstream collection as SF Symbols 8.0; this is source attribution, not a claim that it is Apple's current release or that the SF Symbols app was run locally.
